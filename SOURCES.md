# Source Atlas

**Seed list; last checked:** 2026-10-28. These are starting points, not an exhaustive bibliography, endorsement, or substitute for source appraisal. Confirm current versions and access before relying on them.

## Comparative and historical research

- Human Relations Area Files, **eHRAF World Cultures**: ethnographic collections organized by culture and subject; access is membership-based. Useful infrastructure, not a complete or neutral representation of all cultures. https://hraf.yale.edu/products/ehraf-world-cultures/index.html
- V-Dem Institute, **Datasets**: comparative political indicators and documentation. Inspect operational definitions, coverage, uncertainty, and version before use. https://www.v-dem.net/data/
- Nobel Prize, **Elinor Ostrom: facts and Nobel lecture**: starting point for commons and institutional diversity; read original scholarship and critiques in context. https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economic-sciences/2009/ostrom/facts/ and https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economic-sciences/2009/ostrom/lecture/

## AI governance, rights, and standards

- UNESCO, **Recommendation on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence** (2021). Intergovernmental normative framework; distinguish recommendation from binding domestic law. https://www.unesco.org/en/legal-affairs/recommendation-ethics-artificial-intelligence
- OECD, **AI Principles** (adopted 2019, updated 2024). Read the principles and implementation material; not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific law. https://oecd.ai/en/ai-principles
- NIST, **AI Risk Management Framework** (2023) and its official resource pages. Voluntary framework; assess scope and version. https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework
- Council of Europe, **Framework Convention on Artificial Intelligence and Human Rights, Democracy and the Rule of Law** (CETS 225). Check ratification, entry-into-force, and implementation status at research time. https://www.coe.int/en/web/artificial-intelligence/the-framework-convention-on-artificial-intelligence
- EU, **Artificial Intelligence Act**, Regulation (EU) 2024/1689. Consult the official legal text and current implementation/timeline information. https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj/eng

## Work, participation, and well-being

- International Labour Organization, **Social dialogue**: institutions and processes of tripartite and bipartite consultation; assess local context and worker representation. https://www.ilo.org/topics-and-sectors/social-dialogue
- OECD, **How's Life? / Well-being framework**: multidimensional measures of current and future well-being. Check latest edition and indicator definitions. https://www.oecd.org/en/topics/sub-issues/measuring-well-being-and-progress.html
- UNDP, **Human Development Reports and data**: human development measures and methodological documentation. https://hdr.undp.org/data-center

## Research practice

For every citation, record the exact item used, date accessed, version, source type, relevant evidence, and limitations in `templates/source-record.md`. Follow `RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md`. Prefer primary texts for institutional rules and authoritative current sources for law or standards; seek independent scholarship and affected-party perspectives for interpretation and impact.
