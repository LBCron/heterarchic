## Evidence and method
- [ ] Question, scope, case selection, dates, and methods are stated.
- [ ] Material claims trace to sources; quotations and current facts verified.
- [ ] Counterevidence, critiques, null/failed cases, and uncertainty considered.
- [ ] Sources are not falsely presented as independent corroboration.
- [ ] Cultural, language, access, and dataset limitations stated.

## Governance and impact
- [ ] Affected groups, decision rights, power, distributional effects, and missing voices considered.
- [ ] Rights, privacy, security, labor, sustainability, and remedies assessed where relevant.
- [ ] Normative premises are explicit; alternatives and trade-offs described.
- [ ] Legal or safety claims received qualified review, or absence of review is disclosed.

## Claims and release
- [ ] Findings, interpretations, hypotheses, and recommendations are distinguished.
- [ ] No claim exceeds what the evidence supports; no framework or test is treated as proof of real-world effectiveness.
- [ ] Limitations, residual risks, correction contact/process, and version are present.
